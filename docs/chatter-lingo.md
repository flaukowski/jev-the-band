# Crowd chatter seed lingo

Raw jam-band fan vocabulary used as seed material for `content/chatter/*.json` (built into `public/chatter.json` by `scripts/build-chatter.mjs`). Collected from fan glossaries, forum thread titles (phish.net, PhantasyTour, dead.net "Glossary for the Uninitiated"), Wikipedia (Taper, Shakedown Street vending area), band sites and general scene knowledge. Definitions are paraphrased.

## General jam scene
- wook - road-worn festival lifer; moocher or beloved road dog depending who says it
- wookie / wook flu - the lifer; the cold you catch after a run
- custy / custie - clueless fan who overpays for everything
- heady - high quality, connoisseur grade (heady glass, heady jam)
- kind - good, generous (kind veggie burrito, kind bud)
- schwag - low quality anything
- dank - very good
- crunchy / crispy - funky groove; clean recording
- spun - too altered to function

- segue - > (into) and -> (true seamless segue)
- sandwich - Song A > Song B > Song A
- tease / quote - hinting another song's melody mid-jam
- bustout - song returning after a long absence
- gap - number of shows since a song was last played
- debut - first time played
- rarity / one-timer - almost never played
- flub - botched composed section
- ripcord - abruptly killing a jam to start another song
- standalone - song played without a segue
- opener / closer / set break / encore / double encore
