# What would make the band more interesting

These are proposed directions, not extra commitments or approved changes to the founding requirements. Record user reactions and accepted choices in the prompt/decision logs.

**2026-09-19 update:** the first revision now delivers causal listening, independent phrase commitments, own-motif transformations, swing/tuplets/32nds, sampled guitar/bass/piano/drum accents, guitar articulation, independent visible pedalboards, local soundboard controls, note-driven animation and camera navigation. The dated decision log lists the twelve delivered additions. Items below describe further depth; basic versions of several are now implemented.

## Next: listen, remember, answer

**Revision 0.3 supersedes template transformation:** live notes now come from sequential Jev event decisions, with probability sampling disclosed in traces. Motif memory below should guide newly chosen notes rather than reinstate the procedural live compiler. RMS-matched drive and per-instrument compression are implemented.

1. **Motif memory:** retain a small named motif ledger; let a player quote another player's phrase, answer it, invert it, or bring it back much later. Jev chooses these transformations; code performs them exactly.
2. **Audible invitations:** explicit rhythmic/harmonic proposals persist for two phrases. Peers can adopt, complement, defer, or counter-propose. Show a subtle connection between the initiating and answering player in the stage UI.
3. **Measured novelty:** track rhythmic and melodic distance, not just the word “vary.” Feed stagnation and overcrowding into persona state. Test whether a small perturbation leads to a musical cascade instead of unrelated changes.
4. **Solo conversation:** give two soloists complementary registers, short call/response windows, and cooldown memory. Preserve the ability for solos to overlap; do not make every solo a centrally assigned turn.
5. **Tension and release:** a shared tension indicator derived from harmony, register, dynamics, and density can guide a return to a prior motif. Avoid scripting a fixed intro/build/drop template into every jam.

## Then: richer instruments

- Human-feeling microtiming, swing, articulation and velocity contours inside the shared clock.
- Guitar bends, slides, muted strokes and feedback gestures; bass slides and dead notes; explicit per-hit drum articulation.
- More convincing piano/Rhodes/organ and physical guitar synthesis, or redistributable sample sets with documented licenses. Keep left/right hand voice limits and register feasibility.
- Per-hand register/spread bounds, sustain-pedal semantics, organ drawbars, synth controls, stereo effects and sidechain-aware mix balance.
- A dynamic harmonic grammar: borrowed chords, pedal points, pivot chords and modal interchange, with a clear proposal/adoption boundary.

## Then: a venue people can return to

- Complete event recording, versioned replay, MIDI and WAV exports, and an offline renderer.
- Named jams with reproducible seed and exact provider traces. Distinguish a new live performance from replay.
- A dedicated public broadcaster and verified ChatGPT Site frontend. One band, many listeners.
- Audience suggestions between jams; optional restrained nudges during a jam only after an explicit product decision. Avoid turning every viewer into a conductor.
- A richer character-art pass, more expressive Lux operator animation, better camera composition, and lighting presets with physically legible fixture families.
- A listening rubric: continuity, space, peer response, transitions, novelty, and ending quality. Automatic audio gates remain separate from taste judgments.
