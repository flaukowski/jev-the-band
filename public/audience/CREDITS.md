## What this replaced, and why

The previous bank was generated on **2026-09-20 on the free plan**, which granted **noncommercial use only, with attribution required**. That was fine for a project nobody carried, and it became the blocking question the moment the band was proposed for broadcast on Kannaka TV. Rather than argue the edge of a free-tier grant, the bank was regenerated under a paid plan. The superseded recordings are not in this directory and must not be restored to it.

## Provenance

`manifest.json` records provider, model, duration, mood, kind and SHA-256 for each recording, and each file's hash is re-verified at promotion. Generation prompts, provider account details and billing are deliberately excluded from the public manifest and retained in the private approval record.

## What "approved" means in this bank

The 24 clips were **approved as a batch by the repository owner**, on structural validation only: every file parsed as 2-channel 44.1 kHz 128 kbps CBR MP3, durations matched the manifest to within a frame, and all 24 SHA-256 hashes verified. **No per-clip listening review was performed.** That is a weaker warrant than the approval flag usually carries here, and it is written down rather than implied, because technical validation is not human listening approval.
